Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Willowleaf Labs ("we", "us") is a one-person digital publishing studio selling digital guides (PDF documents) at willowleaflabs.com. Contact: hello@willowleaflabs.com.

2. What we collect

  • Order data. When you buy a product, checkout is processed by our payment provider (e.g., Creem) acting as merchant of record. We receive from them only what is needed to deliver your purchase and support you: your email address, the product purchased, and the order date. We never see or store your card details.
  • Delivery data. Purchased files are delivered as instant downloads at checkout or by email.
  • Website logs. This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and operational purposes.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly ask to be notified of updates.
  • We do not collect more data than listed above — this is a static site with no accounts, no cookies for tracking, and no comment sections.

4. Email communication

If you email us (e.g., support or refund requests), we keep your message and reply for up to 24 months to handle your request and keep records, then delete them.

5. Update emails

Buyers of our guides may receive occasional emails when a purchased title receives a free content update. These are transactional (delivering what was promised with your purchase); you can opt out at any time by replying "no updates".

6. Third-party processors

Our payment/checkout providers and website host process data under their own privacy policies (Creem: creem.io; GitHub Pages: github.com/site/privacy). When you use their checkout, their policy applies to the payment process.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion (subject to record-keeping obligations like receipts) by emailing hello@willowleaflabs.com. We respond within 14 days. If you are in the EU/EEA or California, this serves as our mechanism for access, deletion, and opt-out requests under GDPR and CCPA respectively.

8. Refunds

Digital products carry a 30-day, no-questions-asked refund guarantee. Email hello@willowleaflabs.com with your order ID.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated date above changes and the current version is always available at this URL.
